aWardrobe, the new look

Phase 3 mockups, draft 1. Each screen is 390 by 844, the size of an iPhone. The garments are drawn shapes standing in for your real cut-outs. Nothing here is built yet; it is a picture of the direction.

Three directions, one recommended

The brief wanted a fresh look, so here are three ways it could go. The screens below follow the first. Say if you would rather see the second or third worked up instead.

Pattern paper Recommended

The dressmaker's table: pattern-cutting paper with its faint dot grid, blue ink, and the yellow of a tape measure for the one thing you are acting on. Cut-outs sit on the paper with a soft shadow, like garments laid out. One type family, Bricolage Grotesque, in a display cut for titles and a text cut for everything else.

Lookbook

A fashion lookbook: pure white, black type, an italic serif for titles, one coral accent, and cut-outs shown large with almost no interface around them. Beautiful for browsing, quieter for the editor, where tools need to stand out.

Night studio

Dark first, like a photo studio: near-black surfaces so cut-outs glow, a violet accent, a geometric display face. Strong on a phone at night, and the editor feels like a tool. Light mode becomes the second-class theme.
